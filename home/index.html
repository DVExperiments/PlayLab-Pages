Welcome to the Futures PlayLab

The Futures PlayLab is an academyEX space for hands-on experimentation, creative practice and critical engagement with emerging technologies. Developed alongside the Master of Technological Futures (MTF), it's where ideas about technological futures become things you can try, test and learn from.

You don't need to be a technical expert, and you don't need a finished project. Bring your curiosity, your questions and your context, and start exploring.
