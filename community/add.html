Community page entry helper

Turn form responses into lines you can paste into the Community page. Copy rows from your private sheet, or type one entry in, then copy the result into community/index.html on GitHub.

Nothing you paste here is saved or sent anywhere. It stays in this browser tab.

1What are you adding?

Choose the kind of entry.

2Add the details

In your sheet, select the heading row and the rows you want to add, copy them (Ctrl+C, or Cmd+C on a Mac) and paste them here. Column headings can be the form's own questions.

3Copy into the page
