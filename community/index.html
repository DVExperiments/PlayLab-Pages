      How we look after this space

      • Manaaki each otherBe kind, generous and curious. Assume good intent and disagree with ideas, not people.
      • Ask before you shareGet permission before posting someone else's work, words or photo.
      • Credit the makersName everyone who worked on a piece, including any AI tools you used.
      • Keep it in the whānauWhat's shared here stays here. Don't repost it elsewhere without asking.

      The Portal

      Our virtual space in FrameVR, for meeting up, presenting and exploring together from wherever you are.

      Step into the PlayLab space.

      FrameVR runs in your web browser on a laptop, phone or VR headset. There's nothing to install.

      Enter the space

      First time in FrameVR?

      1. Open the linkUse Chrome, Edge or Safari. On a headset, open the link in the headset's browser.
      2. Choose a name and avatarUse the name your cohort knows you by so people can find you.
      3. Allow your microphoneYour browser will ask. You can mute any time.
      4. Move aroundUse WASD or the arrow keys on a laptop, or drag on a phone. Walk up to people to talk to them.

        Posting tips
